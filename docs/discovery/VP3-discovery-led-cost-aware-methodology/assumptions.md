## Promotion rules

- Observations require reproducible provenance.
- Inferences identify their reasoning boundary.
- Assumptions require an owner, impact, and invalidation trigger.
- Human decisions are attributable and cannot be generated by an agent.
- Unknown evidence blocks completion unless explicitly waived by the applicable
  authority.
